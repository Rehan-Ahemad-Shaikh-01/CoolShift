Assumptions and sources
  • Transfer estimate. The load shape is the IIIT-Delhi academic building. Delhi is not served by MSEDCL, so the prices are for an equivalent building at a Maharashtra site on LT-Commercial. Re-run with that site's meter data before quoting a payback.
  • Tariff. MERC multi-year tariff order of 28 Mar 2025 (Case 217/2024) as revised by order of 25 Mar 2026 (Case 75/2025). Solar window 09:00–17:00 rebate, evening peak 17:00–24:00 surcharge of 25% (LT-Commercial), off-peak at base price. Rebates are 15% (Apr–Sep) and 25% (Oct–Mar) in FY2025–26 and FY2026–27 and step up from FY2027–28.
  • Check against your bill. Confirm the consumer category (LT-Commercial or another) and that a time-of-day meter applies.
  • Energy only. Fixed and demand charges, wheeling, fuel adjustment, duty and tax are excluded.

Why a retrofit layer

BEE's ECSBC 2024 (section 3.2.5) applies to new HVAC equipment; existing systems are exempt. Buildings use about 33% of India's electricity (Challenge 02 brief), so the installed base needs an add-on route to savings.

First reference: IIIT-Delhi Academic Building, with I-BLEND 2014–2017 mains, Wi-Fi and calendar data. To transfer, recalibrate weather (Navi Mumbai next), occupancy, AHU mapping, comfort rules and tariff.
